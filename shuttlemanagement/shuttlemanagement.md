# shuttlemanagement

The **Shuttle Management** feature helps dispatchers manage shuttle routes on the mobile app. Select your shuttle, start the route, and scan parcels. This ensures all scanned parcels are expected at the current site.

### Getting Started

Prerequisites:
* **Nomadia Delivery** mobile application installed.
* Active login credentials.
* Assigned shuttle route.

1. Log into the **Nomadia Delivery** mobile application.

![Frame at 0:10](shuttlemanagement-shuttlemanagement_timestamp_0_to_10.png "Login Screen – The user logs into the mobile application.")

2. Tap on the **Shuttle** option.

![Frame at 0:18](shuttlemanagement-shuttlemanagement_timestamp_0_to_18.png "Menu Screen – Tap the Shuttle option from the main menu.")

### Feature Overview

* **Shuttle**: Opens the shuttle selection list. Use this to choose your assigned route.

![Frame at 0:18](shuttlemanagement-shuttlemanagement_timestamp_0_to_18.png "Shuttle Menu – Tap the Shuttle option to view routes.")

* **Apply**: Confirms your shuttle selection. This registers your choice in the system.

![Frame at 0:23](shuttlemanagement-shuttlemanagement_timestamp_0_to_23.png "Apply Selection – Tap Apply to save your shuttle selection.")

* **Start**: Initiates the route and begins the scanning process. Use this to begin loading parcels.

![Frame at 0:32](shuttlemanagement-shuttlemanagement_timestamp_0_to_32.png "Start Route – Tap Start to initiate the scanning process.")

* **Parcel Scanner**: Scans individual package barcodes. Use this to verify if the parcel is expected at your site.

![Frame at 0:36](shuttlemanagement-shuttlemanagement_timestamp_0_to_36.png "Parcel Scanner – Scan the parcel using the camera interface.")

### How To: Manage Shuttle and Scan Parcels

1. Tap on **Shuttle** from the main menu.

![Frame at 0:18](shuttlemanagement-shuttlemanagement_timestamp_0_to_18.png "Shuttle Menu – Tap the Shuttle option.")

2. Select the designated shuttle from the list.

![Frame at 0:23](shuttlemanagement-shuttlemanagement_timestamp_0_to_23.png "Shuttle List – Choose the correct shuttle route.")

3. Tap on **Apply**.

![Frame at 0:28](shuttlemanagement-shuttlemanagement_timestamp_0_to_28.png "Apply Selection – Confirm your chosen shuttle.")

4. Tap the selected shuttle row.

![Frame at 0:32](shuttlemanagement-shuttlemanagement_timestamp_0_to_32.png "Shuttle Details – Tap the selected shuttle row.")

5. Tap on **Start**.

![Frame at 0:36](shuttlemanagement-shuttlemanagement_timestamp_0_to_36.png "Start Scan – Tap Start to initiate parcel scanning.")

6. Scan the parcel.

![Frame at 0:45](shuttlemanagement-shuttlemanagement_timestamp_0_to_45.png "Scan Parcel – Scan the parcel's barcode.")

#### Troubleshooting

If you receive an error popup during scanning:

![Flow 0:36 to 0:56](shuttlemanagement-shuttlemanagement_timestamp_0_to_36_to_0_to_56.gif "Scan Failure Flow – The user scans a package and receives a popup warning that it is not expected at the current site.")

* **Error**: The package is not expected.
* **Cause**: The scanned parcel is not available at this site.
* **Action**: Verify that you are scanning the correct package.

### Productivity Tips

* ⚠️ **Unexpected Package Warning**: Scanning parcels not on-site triggers a "not expected" popup error.
